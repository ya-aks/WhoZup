Questions

Is WhoZup free?

Yes. Everything in the app is free to use. If you love it, you can leave an optional tip to support development.

Do my friends need the same phone as me?

No. Groups work across phones, so everyone can join no matter what they use.

What counts as a habit?

Anything you want to do after an alarm: a workout, a study session, taking meds, reading, skincare, meditation. Add up to 3 steps, each with a tiny version for days when the full thing is too much.

Can my group see my habit details?

Only if you want them to. Turn off "show my step names" and your group just sees how many steps you finished.

Will the alarm ring if my phone is locked or silent?

Yes. WhoZup uses real system alarms, so it rings like any other alarm on your phone.

What's the difference between an Alarm and a Plan?

An Alarm is yours: your time, your habit, and your group sees how you did. A Plan is shared: one time for everyone, with RSVPs, a live headcount and an optional alarm on everyone's phone.
