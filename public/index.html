Wake Up & Show Up Together.

WhoZup is the real-time social alarm and meetup app designed for friends, workout partners, and study groups to keep each other accountable every morning.

Download for iOS Download for Android

Three Modes for Every Routine

Built for healthy habits, shared accountability, and instant coordination.

⏰

1. Check-ins

Keep your own schedule while staying accountable to friends or partners. When your alarm rings, your status updates live: Woke up, Snoozed, or Silent / Late.

⚡

2. In Sync

One shared alarm set by an admin that rings on everyone's phone simultaneously. Perfect for morning workout crews, study sessions, carpools, or daily standups across time zones.

🎉

3. Events

Organize single-off meetups without group chat chaos. Members reply with a single tap: Coming, Running Late, or Can't Make It with 1-tap admin pings.
